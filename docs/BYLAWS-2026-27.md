## Article VI — Schedules / Division winner / Standings tie breakers (verbatim)
- SECTION 1: The divisions will play a double round robin with division games only counting to division record. Division mates will play each other twice home and away.
- SECTION 2: A team shall be awarded **3 points for a win, 1 point for a tie**. The division placement/standings will be the **order of team points**. The team with the greatest number of points will be declared the champion and higher in the standings. If there is a tie at the top both teams shall be declared division champions.
- SECTION 3: If tied in points work to bring one team out (once one is out start again through the tie breakers). **Better head-to-head record** (among the tied teams).
- SECTION 4: If still tied, the team with the **greater number of wins in division play** shall have higher standings.
- SECTION 5: If still tied, the team with the **least goals given up between head to head teams tied**.
- SECTION 6: If still tied, **goal differential between head to head teams tied**.
- SECTION 7: If still tied, a **coin flip** shall determine standings position (we cannot compute this: render such teams as tied, with a footnote).

## Article VII — CCS representatives (verbatim essentials)
- SECTION 1: 16-team CCS tournament: **SCVAL 7**, BVAL 4, PCAL 2, at-large 3.
- SECTION 2: The **first three teams in each division** are awarded automatic qualifiers (AQ) to CCS playoffs (ties broken by Article VI §2-7). **Fourth place teams play a play-in game; the winner receives the SCVAL 7th AQ.** The losing 4th-place team and both 5th-place teams are submitted to CCS for at-large consideration. #1 v #1, #2 v #2, #3 v #3 crossover games are played after the season to help CCS ordering (home site by coin flip; does not count toward max contests). Per the official schedule PDFs these crossover/play-in games are **Friday, October 30, 2026**.

## Article IV — Game format (for rendering OT)
- Varsity: four 15-minute quarters. After a regulation tie (league AND non-league varsity games): ONE 7-minute sudden-victory period, 7-a-side. If still tied after that one period, the game **ends in a tie** (no shootout in league play). Games start 4:00 pm (3:30 pm from the second Monday-week of October).
- Running clock once a 5-goal differential is reached.
